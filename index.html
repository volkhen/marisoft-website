Welcome to Marisoft

Marisoft is a small collection of practical software utilities created to solve specific problems simply and efficiently.

The programs are designed to be lightweight, understandable and useful — the kind of software you can download, run and get on with your work.

Thunderbird Archive Viewer

A lightweight viewer for old Thunderbird MBOX mail archives.

Browse large mailboxes without importing them back into Thunderbird. Includes fast indexing, HTML message display, attachment access and original EML export.

Version:0.8.2
Platform:Windows x64
License:Freeware
Status:Development release

About Marisoft

Marisoft develops small utilities for everyday technical and computer tasks. The emphasis is on focused functionality, modest system requirements and straightforward interfaces.

This website intentionally follows the compact, information-first style of software websites from the early 2000s.
